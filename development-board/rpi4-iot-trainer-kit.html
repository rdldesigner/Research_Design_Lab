RPI 4 Model B IoT Trainer Kit

Order Code: RDL922

RPI 4 IoT Development Trainer Kit essential development features a plug-and-play design that makes it easy for connections and helps Students, hobbyists, enthusiasts and professionals to focus more on Program/application development. RPI 4 IoT Trainer Kit equipped with onboard IO's, communication interfaces & peripherals. It is really easy to design, experiment with, and test circuits without soldering. It's used in many educational institutions and R&D LAB across the world.
