Start Your Embedded System Design Journey Today..!

Atmega 328 is one of the most commonly used Microcontrollers with an open source platform amongst many hobbyist and industrial communities. The simplicity and the low power of Atmega 328 helps design many prototype boards which could be used in numerous applications. The Atmega 328 includes 6 analog inputs, 14 digital I/O pins (6 amongst these could be used as PWM outputs), a crystal oscillator with 16MHz frequency.
