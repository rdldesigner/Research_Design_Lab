8051 Development Board - Trainer Kit

Order Code: RDL847

The 8051 Development Board - Trainer Kit features a plug-and-play design that makes it easy for connections and helps students, hobbyists, enthusiasts and professionals to focus more on program/application development. The board is equipped with onboard IO's, communication interfaces & peripherals. It is really easy to design, experiment with, and test circuits without soldering. It's used in many educational institutions and R&D LABs across the world.

Board Features

  • Plug & Play Interface Connectivity
  • Professional EMI/RFI Compliant PCB Layout Design
  • Modular Block design makes Easy access & quick Prototyping
  • FRC connectivity features minimize the connection Error
  • High Quality Grade PCB with Wooden Enclosure
  • The DIP40 locking device for reuse ATMEL microcontroller
  • Supports 8051, AT89SXX and AT89CXX series chips 40 pin DIP package
  • On Board Programming
  • 8 interfacing LED's
  • 1 x 4 Menu keypad
  • 4 x 4 Matrix Keypad
  • RS232, RS485, USB communication port
  • 16 x 2 LCD & OLED Display
  • 8 bit 4 port IO
  • On Board WiFi/Bluetooth Connectivity
  • 3.3 to 5V Level Converter
  • Power Supply 3.3V and 5V
  • SD Card Interface
  • RTC & EEPROM Interface
  • DC Motor / Stepper Motor Driver
  • Relay, Buzzer
  • 1 x Temperature Sensor
  • 3 x Analog Test POT
