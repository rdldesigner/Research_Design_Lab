ESP32 IoT Development Trainer Kit

Order Code: RDL814

The ESP32 IoT Development Trainer Kit features a plug-and-play design that makes it easy for connections and helps students, hobbyists, enthusiasts and professionals to focus more on program/application development. The board is equipped with onboard IO's, communication interfaces & peripherals. It is really easy to design, experiment with, and test circuits without soldering. It's used in many educational institutions and R&D LABs across the world.

Specifications

MCU

  • ESP32-D0WD-V3 embedded, Xtensa® dual-core 32-bit LX6 microprocessor, up to 240 MHz
  • 448 KB ROM for booting and core functions
  • 520 KB SRAM for data and instructions
  • 16 KB SRAM in RTC
  • 4 MB SPI flash

Wi-Fi

  • 802.11b/g/n
  • Bit Rate: 802.11n up to 150 Mbps
  • A-MPDU and A-MSDU Aggregation
  • 0.4 µs Guard interval support
  • Center frequency range of operating channel: 2412 ~ 2484 MHz

Bluetooth® / BLE

  • Bluetooth V4.2 BR/EDR and Bluetooth LE Specification
  • Class-1, Class-2 and Class-3 Transmitter
  • AFH
  • CVSD and SBC
