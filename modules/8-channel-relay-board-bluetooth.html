8 Channel Relay Board - Bluetooth

Order Code: RDL/8RB/14/001/V1.0

This is a Bluetooth-controlled relay board featuring 8 SPDT relays rated up to 7A each. It enables wireless control of devices up to 230V/120V through serial data transmission. The device is suitable for home automation, hobby projects, and industrial automation applications.

Key Features

  • 8 SPDT relay channels (7A 250V, 12A 120V, 10A 125VAC, 10A 28VDC)
  • Power supply: 12VDC 2AMP with 1A current consumption
  • LED indicators for relay and power status
  • ULN2803 IC-based driver design
  • FR4 Grade PCB with FPT certification
  • NodeMCU firmware included

Wireless Specifications

  • Bluetooth: V4.2 BR/EDR and LE with Class-1/2/3 transmitter support, AFH, CVSD and SBC codecs
  • Wi-Fi: 802.11b/g/n up to 150 Mbps with A-MPDU/A-MSDU aggregation
  • MCU: ESP32-D0WD-V3 with dual-core 32-bit LX6 processor (240 MHz), 448KB ROM, 520KB SRAM, 4MB SPI flash

ESP32 Features

MCU

  • ESP32-D0WD-V3 embedded, Xtensa® dual-core 32-bit LX6 microprocessor, up to 240 MHz
  • 448 KB ROM for booting and core functions
  • 520 KB SRAM for data and instructions
  • 16 KB SRAM in RTC
  • 4 MB SPI flash

Wi-Fi

  • 802.11b/g/n
  • Bit rate: 802.11n up to 150 Mbps
  • A-MPDU and A-MSDU aggregation
  • 0.4 µs guard interval support
  • Center frequency range of operating channel: 2412 ~ 2484 MHz

Security

  • WFA, WPA/WPA2 and WAPI

Relay SwitchBoard Software

The 8-RelaySwitchBoard Android app pairs with the board over Bluetooth, lets you assign custom names to each relay channel, and provides a simple ON/OFF switchboard to control connected loads.
