Isolated Serial USB to RS232 Adapter

Order Code: RDL898

An industrial-grade adapter enabling communication between serial RS232 devices and USB-connected systems. Both ports are completely isolated, with ESD protection and electromagnetic compliance for reliable operation in harsh industrial environments. Powered directly from USB, it uses the FT232 chip for high-speed, reliable data transfer.

Key Features

  • Both ports completely isolated
  • ESD protected on both serial and USB sides
  • EMC/EMI compliance enabled design
  • USB-powered; no external supply needed
  • FT232 chip for high-speed data transfer
  • Industrial-grade, compact, rugged construction
  • High common-mode transient immunity: 100 kV/µs typical
  • Minimum data rate of 120 KBps
  • UL recognition: 3000 Vrms per UL 1577
  • RoHS-compliant and AEC-Q101 certified
