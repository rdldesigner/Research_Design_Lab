8 Channel Relay Board - Serial

Order Code: RDL/8RB-S/14/001/V1.0

A serially-controlled relay module featuring 8 SPDT relays rated at 7A each, enabling direct control of multiple 230V/120V devices. Ideal for automation and hobby projects.

Key Features

  • 8 SPDT relay channels (7A 250V, 12A 120V, 10A 125VAC, 10A 28VDC)
  • 12VDC 2AMP power supply
  • 400mA current consumption
  • ULN2803 IC driver design
  • TTL output with status LEDs
  • High-quality FR4 PCB with FPT certification

Specifications

SpecificationDetails

Applications

  • Home automation
  • Hobby projects
  • Industrial automation

I/O Connections

Arduino

Connect the Arduino Uno's Tx/Rx pins and 5V/GND to the relay board's serial header to control all 8 relays.

Raspberry Pi

Connect Raspberry Pi GPIO14 (TXD), GPIO7 (CE1) and ground to the relay board's TX, +5V and GND pins respectively.

BeagleBone

Connect BeagleBone Serial5 TX/GPIO2_14 (P9 pin 37) and GND (P9 pin 45) to the relay board's TX, +5V and GND pins.
