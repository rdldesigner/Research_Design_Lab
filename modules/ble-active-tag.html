BLE Active Tag

Order Code: RDL936

A compact, battery-powered wireless device utilizing Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE 5.0) for real-time asset tracking, personnel monitoring, and industrial automation. Designed for factories, warehouses, hospitals, campuses, and logistics facilities, it delivers scalable and secure wireless communication with ultra-low power consumption and easy integration with IoT platforms.

Key Features

  • Ultra-low power consumption
  • Low deployment cost
  • Easy integration with IoT platforms
  • Scalable for thousands of assets
  • Secure wireless communication
  • BLE 5.0 with 2.4 GHz ISM band
  • Transmission range up to 100 m (open space)
  • Long battery life: 2–5 years (CR2477 / CR2032)
  • Configurable beacon interval: 100 ms – 10 s
  • Industrial-grade ABS enclosure
  • Flexible mounting: adhesive / screw / keychain
