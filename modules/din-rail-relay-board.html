DIN Rail Relay Board

Order Code: RDL913

DIN Rail Relay Board with 2/4/8/16 SPDT relays featuring isolated digital IO, controlled from any PLC, Arduino & Raspberry Pi. Suitable for home automation applications, hobby projects, and industrial automation.

Key Features

  • Completely opto isolated relay module
  • LED indication for relay status & power supply
  • SPDT Relay (7A 250V, 12A 120V, 10A 125VAC, 10A 28VDC)
  • Relay Module Power supply selection available in 5V / 12V / 24V
  • Greater Power handling PCB 70 Micron with good quality screw Connector
  • -40°C to 85°C extended operating temperature range

Block Diagram

Each relay channel is driven through an optocoupler for isolation, with shared VCC, GND, common and individual input lines for every relay.

I/O Connections

Arduino

Connect the Arduino Uno's digital I/O pins to the IN1/IN2 inputs of each relay channel, along with shared VCC and GND, to switch connected lights and fans.

PLC

Connect a PLC's output terminals to the IN1/IN2 inputs along with VCC and GND to control relay channels switching loads such as solenoid valves.

Raspberry Pi

Connect Raspberry Pi GPIO pins to the IN1/IN2 inputs along with VCC and GND to control the relay channels and switch connected loads.
