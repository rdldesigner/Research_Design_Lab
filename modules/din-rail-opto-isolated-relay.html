DIN Rail Opto Isolated Relay

Order Code: RDL775

The DIN Rail Opto Isolated Relay enables remote control of electrical equipment via web and mobile applications. It can switch AC or DC high voltage, high current loads working at 110V or 220V AC mains such as lights, fans, motors, and any electrical equipment. Designed for DIN rail mounting, it provides four isolated relay channels ideal for industrial control panels and automation systems.

Key Features

  • Four high-quality SPDT relays with normally open and normally closed contacts
  • LED indicators for each relay channel and power supply status
  • 5V microcontroller direct input compatibility
  • 7A – 10A current capacity
  • AC / DC operating voltage compatibility (110V / 220V AC mains)
  • Zero leakage current design
  • Screw terminal block connections
  • Onboard DC-DC converter
  • Current feedback diode protection
  • High-quality PCB construction
  • DIN Rail mounting compatible

  • Industrial automation and control panel switching
