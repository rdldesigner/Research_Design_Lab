8 Channel Relay Board - RS485

Order Code: RDL/8RB-R/14/001/V1.0

This relay module features eight channels controlled via the RS485 protocol. It contains 8 SPDT relays rated at 7A each, enabling direct control of up to eight 230V/120V devices. Ideal for home automation, hobby projects, and industrial automation applications.

Key Features

  • 8 SPDT relay channels (7A 250V, 12A 120V, 10A 125VAC, 10A 28VDC)
  • 12VDC 2AMP power supply
  • 400mA current consumption
  • ULN2803 IC driver design
  • RS485-based communication interface
  • LED indicators for relay and power status
  • High-quality FR4 PCB with FPT certification
