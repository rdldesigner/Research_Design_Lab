4-20mA Current Loop Transmitter

Order Code: RDL825

Precision current output converters designed to transmit analog 4-to-20mA signals over an industry standard current loop, with accurate scaling and output limiting capabilities.

Key Features

  • Low quiescent current: 200µA
  • 5V regulator for external circuits
  • VREF for sensor excitation (XTR116): 4.096V
  • Low span error: 0.05%
  • Low nonlinearity error: 0.003%
  • Wide loop supply range: 7.5V to 36V
  • SO-8 package
  • Industrial temperature range: −40°C to +125°C
