Isolated USB to RS485 Adapter

Order Code: RDL904

An industrial-grade adapter enabling communication between serial RS485 devices and USB-connected systems. Both ports are completely isolated, with ESD protection and electromagnetic compliance for reliable operation in harsh industrial environments.

Key Features

  • Complete port isolation on both connections
  • ESD and EMI/EMC protection
  • USB-powered (no external supply needed)
  • FT232 chip for high-speed data transfers
  • Supports 32 slaves on RS485 bus
  • Operating temperature: -40°C to 85°C
  • UL recognition: 3000 Vrms for 1 minute per UL 1577
  • RoHS-compliant SOIC packaging
  • Transparent USB connector with TX/RX indicator LEDs
  • Supports standard COM port emulation and D2XX drivers
