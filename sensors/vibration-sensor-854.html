Vibration Sensor

Order Code: RDL850

The device monitors and analyzes vibrations in industrial and commercial environments, providing valuable insights into equipment health, allowing for preventive maintenance and improved operational efficiency.

Key Advantages

  • Proactive alerts reduce downtime
  • Minimizes unplanned downtime and maintenance expenses
  • Condition-based maintenance increases reliability and uptime
  • Early problem detection improves safety and extends motor lifespan
  • Remote real-time monitoring enhances operational efficiency

Specifications

SpecificationDetails
Sensor Type3-axial MEMS accelerometer
Velocity Range0-25mm/Sec ±10%
Output Options4-20mA / Modbus RTU / Wi-Fi / Bluetooth
Shock Survival10,000g
SensitivityLess than 5%
Settling Time3 Seconds

Connection Diagram

Wiring connection of the Vibration Sensor to a 24V supply and PLC via RS485 (A/B lines).

Applications

Equipment monitoring includes:

  • Manufacturing plants
  • Oil/gas refineries
  • Power facilities
  • Mining operations
  • Automotive assembly lines
  • HVAC systems
  • Wind turbines
  • Gearboxes, pumps, fans, motors, compressors, centrifuges
  • Conveyors, handlers, rolls, dryers, presses, spindles, and process equipment
